Mentions and pings are built directly into the message flow.

They support:#

  • player mentions
  • @Player and exact-name mention behavior
  • custom ping triggers
  • sound notifications
  • actionbar notifications
  • per-player ping toggles
  • staff-friendly ping rules
  • cross-server mention awareness
  • mention formatting based on the mentioned player

Because mentions are part of CoreChatX itself, they can work with formatting, notifications, privacy choices, and Velocity network presence instead of pretending every server is isolated.

/msg and /reply share token rendering on standalone Paper and proxy backends: ChatItems, permission-controlled formatting, keywords and participant mentions. Mention text resolves only the sender or recipient; mention and custom-ping notifications target only the admitted recipient, never the sender, unrelated players or SocialSpy viewers. The receiving backend rechecks the recipient's permissions and preferences. Custom pings still require the sender's Bukkit use-permission; bypass-toggle can override only the recipient's ping toggles. The normal PM bell remains independent.
